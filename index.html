Temperature Transmitter Applications

Temperature transmitters are widely used in industrial processes to monitor and control temperature in applications such as chemical reactors, HVAC systems, food processing, and manufacturing. This transmitter has a span of 368°F (from 32°F to 400°F), making it suitable for various industrial heating processes. The 4-20mA signal provides reliable transmission over long distances and allows for easy detection of sensor faults (a reading below 4mA indicates a broken wire or sensor failure).
